Part 3 – Pre-Analytical Quality Verification [0:50 – 1:35] 
[Open Quality Verification] 
"This is our ISO 15189 5-Point Quality Verification Gate. It verifies: 
• Barcode legibility, 
• Correct container, 
• Volume adequacy (≥ 3 mL), 
• Specimen integrity (no hemolysis or clots), and 
• Collection window. 
If the barcode is damaged, we can click Print Barcode to reprint without duplicating records. 
If any check fails (e.g., Hemolysis), clicking Reject Sample captures standardized CAP rejection codes 
and triggers an automatic recollection request to the phlebotomist. 
(Click Accept and Queue for Analysis) When all 5 checks pass, clicking Accept transitions the specimen 
directly into the MLT worklist. 
[Briefly mention Logs] 
We also support Accessioning Logs with Excel Export for daily audit reporting."

Part 4 – MLT Worklist & CDS Result Entry [1:35 – 2:30] 
[Switch to MLT → Sample Worklist → Click Open on a sample] 
"Moving to the Analytical Phase, the MLT Sample Worklist organizes pending tests by STAT priority. 
Opening a sample brings us into Result Entry: As results are entered, our Clinical Decision Support 
Engine evaluates reference ranges and auto-flags values — Normal in green, High in orange, and 
dangerous values trigger CRITICAL HIGH panic alerts. 
Beside the results, an automated Delta-Check Algorithm (Δ% ≥ 40%) compares today's value against the 
patient's historical baseline to intercept specimen mix-ups. 
I also select the analyzer (e.g., Cobas e411) and can add technical notes. In line with ISO 15189, Save 
Draft saves partial work without locking, while Submit for Verification validates all fields and legally 
locks the record for supervisor verification."
